    How it works

    • Press harder with a pen for a thicker line.
    • Two fingers move and zoom the board. Tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo.
    • Tap the zoom percentage in the corner to go back to 100%, and tap it again straight away to show it all.
    • Draw a shape and hold still for half a second at the end: a wobbly line, box, triangle or circle snaps to a clean one. Keep moving and it lets go. The shapes tool drags out exact ones, outlined or filled.
    • The eraser rubs out just where it touches. Pick Whole lines beside it (on a narrow screen, tap the eraser twice) to take away every line it touches instead. Your pen’s eraser end, or its side button held down, erases the same way whatever tool you’re on.
    • Hold the pen button down (or press L) for a red pointer, to point things out as you present: its trail fades a second after you lift, and it’s never kept. Pick any tool to put it away.
    • The lasso picks up lines: draw round them, then drag the dashed box to move them, or its corner to make them bigger or smaller. The buttons by it duplicate them, give them another colour, delete them or copy a picture of them. Tap outside the box to put them down.
    • Add a photo (here, or paste or drop one on the board) to write over a worksheet, a floor plan or a screenshot. It goes under your ink, picked up with the lasso so you can move it or resize it by its corner. The eraser rubs it out like ink; Whole lines takes it only when it touches no lines.
    • Tap a tool twice on a narrow screen for its colours and sizes. The shapes tool shows its shapes every time you tap it.
    • With a keyboard: P pen, H highlighter, S shapes, E eraser, V lasso (Delete deletes what it picked up, Esc puts it down), L pointer (Esc puts it away), 1–6 colours, [ and ] size, F show it all, Ctrl (⌘ on a Mac) + Z undo, Ctrl + Shift + Z or Ctrl + Y redo, Ctrl + 0 back to 100%, Ctrl + = and Ctrl + − zoom, Space + drag to move, Esc closes the menu, the list of boards or a pop-up.
    • Add it to your home screen to use it full screen, with no address bar. It works offline.
